          How to use this map

          1. 1
            Load new data – choose the latest line-list Excel. It is remembered on this computer for next time.
          2. 2
            Filter – pick the area (a block, PHC or village), the period and the disease. The numbers and the map change at once.
          3. 3
            Hotspots – see where cases are grouping. Tap a hotspot to zoom to it and see its cases.
          4. 4
            Download – get a ready PowerPoint, or the case and hotspot lists in Excel.

          Words used

          Hotspot
          A group of cases close together in place and time. The rule is shown at the top of the Hotspots tab and can be changed.
          Active hotspot
          A hotspot with a case in the last 14 days (or the number of days chosen). "Over" means no recent case.
          New this week
          Cases in the 7 days up to the latest date in the file.
          IP Fever
          In-patient fever cases (Fever sheet). Dengue = lab-confirmed dengue (DENGUE sheet).
          Data check
          Rows with missing or doubtful details, such as no location or a date that does not add up.
          Hide names
          Removes names, phone numbers and addresses from the screen and from downloads, for meetings.
